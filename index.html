07 / intellectual architecture

Scientific Method

The visual language should communicate disciplined curiosity, not performative expertise.

01 · QUESTION

What exactly are we trying to explain?

Define the phenomenon before searching for an answer.

02 · EVIDENCE

What observations support the claim?

Prioritize reproducible measurements and primary literature.

03 · MODEL

What mechanism could explain it?

Connect molecular, cellular, neural and population-level processes.

04 · FALSIFICATION

What would prove the idea wrong?

Actively search for counterexamples rather than confirmation alone.

05 · UPDATE

What changes when new evidence arrives?

A scientific position remains revisable when better evidence appears.
